Auto-update for the desktop app, orchestrated entirely on the Rust side.

The Tauri updater plugin exposes download() -> Vec<u8> and install(&[u8]), which is what makes the “remind me later” flow possible: a verified artifact can be written to disk, survive a process restart and be installed on the next launch without asking the endpoint again.
